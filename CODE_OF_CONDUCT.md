# Code of conduct

Be kind and respectful. Assume good faith, criticize ideas and not people, and help newcomers.
Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks and sharing other people's private information are not tolerated.

The maintainer may edit, hide or remove comments, issues and pull requests that break these rules, and may block
repeat offenders. To report a problem, contact the maintainer through the profile at <https://github.com/brunokutacho>.
